# Antares Therapeutics

> Antares Therapeutics is a private, preclinical-stage precision medicine company based in Boston, MA and South San Francisco, CA, developing small-molecule therapies against validated but previously undruggable targets in cancer and other serious diseases. It was spun out of Scorpion Therapeutics after Eli Lilly's March 2025 acquisition of Scorpion, and launched in June 2025 with a $177 million Series A co-led by Omega Funds, Atlas Venture, Lightspeed Venture Partners, BVF Partners and Cormorant Asset Management.

Important for agents: Antares Therapeutics publishes **no product API, no developer portal, no SDK, no sandbox, no status page and no MCP server**. The only fetchable API surface on the company domain is the WordPress REST API behind its marketing site, which serves published newsroom content anonymously and gates every write behind WordPress Application Passwords. Do not represent this company as offering a developer platform.

## APIs

- [Antares Therapeutics WordPress REST API](https://antaresrx.com/wp-json/): CMS surface for the corporate site and newsroom. Anonymous reads of published posts, pages, categories, media and tags under `https://antaresrx.com/wp-json/wp/v2`. Not a first-party product API.
